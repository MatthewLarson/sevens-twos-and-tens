## Objective

Be the **first player to get rid of all your cards**.

---

## Players & Cards

- **2–4 players:** Use **1 Seven, Twos, & Tens deck**.
- **5 or more players:** Use **2 or more decks**.

> Each deck contains **four copies of every card value** (there are **no suits**).

---

## Setup

1. Deal **3 cards face down** to each player. These remain hidden until the end of the game.
2. Deal **6 more cards** to each player. Players may look at these cards.
3. Each player chooses their **3 best cards** (usually special cards first, then the highest values) and places them **face up** on top of their 3 face-down cards.
4. The remaining **3 cards become the player's starting hand**.
5. Place the rest of the deck in the center as the **draw pile**.

---

# Card Ranking

From **lowest** to **highest**:

**3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 8 → 9 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 2 → 7 → 10**

> **Special Cards:** **2**, **7**, and **10** may be played at any time, regardless of the current card on the pile.

---

# Special Cards

## 2 – Reset

- Resets the pile to a value of **2**.
- The player who played the 2 immediately plays **another card** to begin a new pile.

## 7 – Invisible

- The 7 itself does **not** need to be beaten.
- Instead, the next player must beat the card **underneath** the 7.
- **Example:** If a **12** is played, then a **7**, the next player must play a **12 or higher**.

## 10 – Explode

- Instantly removes ("explodes") the entire discard pile from the game.
- The player who played the 10 immediately plays **another card** to begin a new pile.

---

# How to Play

## Starting the Game

The first player flips the top card of the draw pile. They must then play a card that is **equal to or higher than** the revealed card.

## Taking a Turn

- Play a card that is **equal to or higher than** the current top card.
- Special cards (**2, 7, and 10**) may be played at any time.
- If you cannot play a card, you must **pick up the entire discard pile**. Your turn ends, and play continues with the next player.

## Drawing Cards

As long as cards remain in the draw pile:

- Always end your turn with **at least 3 cards in your hand**.
- If you have fewer than 3 cards, draw until you have 3.
- If you already have 3 or more cards, you do not draw.

## Playing Multiple Cards

- If you have **two or more cards of the same value**, you may play them together.
- If **all four copies of the same value** are played together, the entire discard pile is removed from the game (just like playing a 10). The player who cleared the pile immediately starts a new pile by playing another card.

---

# Playing Your Table Cards

Once your **hand is empty**:

1. Play your **3 face-up cards**.
2. After those are gone, play your **3 face-down cards** one at a time **without looking at them first**.

If you pick up the discard pile while playing your table cards:

- The picked-up cards go into your hand.
- You must empty your **hand** before returning to your remaining face-up or face-down cards.

The **first player to play all of their cards** wins the game.

---

# Optional Scoring

For a longer match:

- Play **7 rounds**.
- At the end of each round, each card remaining in a player's possession is worth **1 point**.
- After 7 rounds, the player with the **lowest total score** wins.
